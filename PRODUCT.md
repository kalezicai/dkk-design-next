# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

German-speaking B2B and local-business decision-makers (Geschäftsführer, Marketingleiter, Fuhrparkleiter, Inhaber von Weingütern, Logistikfirmen, Regionalverlagen) who commission print-ready design, vehicle wrapping, branding, and web work. They are risk-averse, research-driven, and compare multiple suppliers before deciding. They buy on evidence (numbers, guarantees, response times, reviews), not on adjectives.

## Product Purpose

DKK design&marketing sells Druckvorstufe (pre-press), Branding, Webentwicklung, Marketing and Printprodukte with fixed prices and a 12-year, zero-rejection track record. Success = the visitor stops comparing, contacts the studio, and commissions a package. The site must feel like a German print shop with a solo-expert voice: precise, technical, trustworthy, and human — the opposite of a generic agency template.

## Brand Personality

Precise, craftsmanlike, German. Three words: präzise, ehrlich, handwerklich. Voice is direct, no hype, numbered proof points, short declarative sentences. "Der Techniker hinter der Vektordatei" — not an account-manager shop.

## Anti-references

- Generic agency landing pages (hero + logos wall + card grids + "Wir machen Marketing!")
- English-language styling on a German B2B site
- Testimonial sliders without named clients and numbers
- Anything that hides the Impressum or the real person behind the studio
- Vague "ab X €" pricing without deliverables lists

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over adjectives — numbers, guarantees and named clients lead every section.
2. Practice what you preach — the site itself must be druckfertig: fast, precise, zero broken elements.
3. Solo-expert trust — one face, one phone line, one person who answers; stated capacity limits instead of promises.
4. Technical beauty — craft details (grids, mono labels, precise spacing) worn like the work itself.
5. Show, don't tell — before/after sliders, real file previews, interactive preflight checks over prose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast on all text
- Full prefers-reduced-motion support (animations collapse to instant)
- Semantic HTML, focus-visible styles, aria states on all interactive elements
- German as the only language on the surface
